Before you fly

Flight briefing

  1. 01

    Search & identify

    Fly into areas A, B, and C. Stay near each marker until the scan ring fills and the swimmer appears.

  2. 02

    Steady your approach

    Keep clear of the rocky islands. The wind nudges your drone and shifts the drop crosshair.

  3. 03

    Release support

    Place the yellow crosshair over a scanned swimmer, then release a ring. You have eight attempts.

How the score works

Each swimmer assisted earns 100 points, plus up to 100 for drop accuracy. Assist all three for a time bonus of up to 150. Each collision costs 15 points; the total never falls below zero.

Move closer after a missed drop and try again. The mission ends when all three swimmers have a ring, time runs out, or all eight rings have landed.

From play to prototype

A window into the real project.

Finding a person, approaching carefully, and delivering support are also central to my rescue-drone work. The project report describes computer-vision detection, flight control, and a flotation-release mechanism.

This educational game uses simplified flight, wind, and rescue mechanics. Its scores and timing are game settings, not measured prototype performance.
